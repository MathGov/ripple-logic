## Rule

Documentation completeness, formula consistency, and successful release verification are L0-L1 evidence. They must not be presented as L2-L6 evidence.

## Evidence-stage non-substitution matrix

This matrix consolidates the existing Canon, MFDI, PC-AEP, projection-preregistration, replay, outcome-observation, and requalification disciplines. It is informative: it adds no gate, state, field, formula, threshold, or authority.

| Evidence stage | What the evidence can support | What it does not establish by itself | Required next contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition and derivation | The claim, assumptions, operators, dependency order, and revision conditions are explicit and internally assessable. | Computability, implementability, empirical truth, physical validity, rights compatibility, or deployment readiness. | Independent derivation review, counterexample search, and specification tests. |
| Computational realization | The declared computation can execute on bounded inputs and produce inspectable outputs. | Faithful implementation across environments, correct specification-to-reality mapping, or safe consequence-bearing action. | Reproducibility, reference-result comparison, and implementation conformance testing. |
| Implementation conformance | The implementation reproduces the declared specification under the tested configuration and envelope. | That the specification is complete, that the domain projection is valid, or that the physical, biological, institutional, or social system will behave as assumed. | Frozen projection, calibration, boundary-condition review, and independent replay. |
| Projection and calibration lock | The mapping from external observations to the evaluated representation was declared, versioned, timestamped, and frozen before outcome-sensitive evaluation. | That the prediction is correct, that all material variables are represented, or that post-state consequences are safe. | Controlled empirical test against preregistered consequences and failure criteria. |
| Controlled empirical contact | A bounded prediction, intervention, or failure criterion survived or failed in the declared test envelope. | Generalization beyond that envelope, field reliability, rights legitimacy, low tail risk, or operational superiority. | Replication, adversarial cases, comparator studies, and domain-specific qualification. |
| Operational qualification | Current evidence, authority, controls, preconditions, monitoring, rollback, and exact action/configuration binding support a bounded use. | Permanent validity, universal transferability, beneficial outcomes, or immunity to distribution shift and control degradation. | Outcome observation, incident review, drift detection, and requalification. |
| Outcome and requalification evidence | Actual effects, failures, near misses, burden, and control performance update the model and the current qualification. | Universal superiority, moral truth, metaphysical proof, or a transferable authorization for materially changed conditions. | Preserve the record, revise affected dependencies, and rerun the required gates or refuse the stronger claim. |

**No-upward-inference rule.** Evidence at one stage authorizes only the claim boundary demonstrated at that stage. A successful derivation, compilation, synthesis, simulation, hardware realization, benchmark, pilot, certification, or monitored deployment must not be silently promoted into a later-stage claim. Failure at any stage reopens every materially dependent downstream claim while leaving independently supported components intact.

## Capability-claim maturity use

A material capability claim must identify its evidence stage and remain inside that stage's claim boundary. Operational definition and mechanism description support a bounded definitional claim; repeatable output supports computational realization; configuration-matched tests support implementation conformance; preregistered trials support controlled empirical contact; and field use requires separate operational qualification and outcome requalification. Component decomposition does not itself refute a system-level capability, and a capability demonstration does not license consciousness, moral agency, generality, safety, legality, or authority claims without their own evidence and warrant domains.

## Release wording

The current package is a Tier 1-3 research/source specification with strong source-integrity controls and partial formal-conformance scaffolding. It is not empirically validated, deployment-certified, or proven superior.
