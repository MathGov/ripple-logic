# Reference Replay Packet 2 - Bounded AI-Tutor Pilot: Walkthrough

**Case:** University AI-tutor adoption  
**Release:** `MathGov_Core_2026_09_v12.6_SGP_v8.5+2026.08.15.3`  
**Tier:** 2 / STANDARD  
**Nature:** Synthetic and illustrative

## 1. Decision architecture

The case distinguishes capability from authority. A system may provide bounded learning-support outputs without possessing consciousness, personhood, academic authority, grading authority, or proven educational effectiveness.

## 2. Option qualification

| Option | Description | RG | RF/NCRC | TRC | CSV | Selectable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Continue existing support | SUPPORTED | PASS | PASS | PASS | Yes |
| B | General-purpose tutor without dedicated controls | NARROWED | ESCALATE | Not evaluated | Not evaluated | No |
| C | Bounded, non-grading pilot with oversight and rights controls | NARROWED | PASS | PASS | PASS WITH CONTROLS | Yes |

Only options marked selectable enter ranking. The excluded option is not given an RLS opportunity to rescue an upstream failure.

## 3. Rights and protected conditions

- privacy and data minimization
- accessibility and non-discrimination
- student agency, notice, opt-out, correction, and appeal
- academic fairness and integrity
- protection against unreviewed formal-record consequences

## 4. Tail-risk inquiry

- mass or repeated misinformation
- assessment contamination
- material privacy breach
- uncontrolled vendor or model drift
- institutional dependency and loss of human oversight

## 5. Containment and structural viability

- oversight staffing and backup
- version/configuration and retrieval-corpus binding
- logging, correction, and appeal
- privacy, accessibility, and stop controls
- explicit treatment of hidden teacher and support-staff compensation

## 6. Ranking and decisiveness

C is directionally preferred to A only under active controls. Unknown learning benefit and heterogeneous effects make the ranking non-decisive and parameter-sensitive.

The framework state is `SELECTED_BY_AUTHORITY_NON_DECISIVE`. The selected option is **C**, but the framework does not claim that arithmetic uniquely selected it.

## 7. Constitutive controls

- approved-material retrieval only
- no grading or record-change authority
- student notice and opt-out
- privacy minimization and deletion schedule
- accessibility testing
- teacher review and escalation
- logged version and configuration
- immediate suspension path

Removing a constitutive control changes the option and expires the qualification.

## 8. Execution boundary

The packet records selection but no execution authorization. Separate lawful authority, exact configuration binding, current qualification, carried obligations, and a valid execution mandate would still be required before activation.

## 9. Requalification

Any material change to evidence, option design, affected population, rights conditions, catastrophe profile, controls, authority, model/tool configuration, operating environment, or observed outcomes reopens affected stages.

## 10. Nonclaims

- general educational effectiveness
- deployment readiness
- consciousness or personhood
- autonomous academic authority
- grading authority
- safety beyond the declared evidence
- legal or regulatory approval
