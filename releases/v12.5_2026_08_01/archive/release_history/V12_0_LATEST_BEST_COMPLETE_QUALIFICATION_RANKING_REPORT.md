# v12.0 Latest Best Complete - Qualification-Before-Ranking Integration Report

## Purpose

This pass integrates one final scientific and teaching improvement into the v12.0 line without changing component versions: RippleLogic is explicitly presented as a two-phase method.

## Accepted improvements

1. **First qualify options, second rank survivors.** RG, RF/NCRC, TRC, and CSV create the selectable set. RLS ranks only that set.
2. **TRC remains separate from CSV.** TRC is the ruin veto. CSV is the viability and control test. They may exchange evidence, but they must not collapse.
3. **Qualification prevents invalid optimization.** RLS must not score ungrounded candidates, rights violations, ruin paths, physically unsupported execution claims, or structurally non-viable options as ordinary alternatives.
4. **Teaching clarity improved.** README, Essentials, Quickstart, Canon, Cascade Standard, CSV Gate Standard, Gate Boundary Guide, Plain-English Summary, Primer, Agent System, PC-AEP, Core Component Map, and Mermaid cascade overview now carry the two-phase framing.

## Rejected changes

- No TRC absorption into CSV.
- No sixth gate.
- No equation change.
- No version bump.
- No claim that MathGov is empirically validated or computes physical safety.

## Canonical wording

First qualify options. Second rank the survivors.

Reality decides what has standing. Rights decide what cannot be traded. Tail risk decides what cannot be gambled. Structural viability decides what can actually stand. Ripple scoring decides what best serves flourishing among the survivors.
