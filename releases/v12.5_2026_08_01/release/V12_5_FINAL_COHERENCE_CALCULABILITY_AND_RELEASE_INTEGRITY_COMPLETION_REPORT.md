## 2. Scientific and conceptual audit

### 2.1 Reality Grounding

The package consistently separates the real-world target from observations, models, representations, evidence, uncertainty, validity domains, and decision authority. Fluent generation, model confidence, simulation output, or internal consistency cannot self-certify correspondence with reality. PC-AEP supplies a governed interface for physical and causal evidence without pretending that MathGov itself is a universal physics engine.

### 2.2 Rights Floor / NCRC

Rights remain non-compensatory and upstream of welfare optimization. Rights-specific evidence channels are separated from ordinary welfare propagation, and unresolved rights evidence cannot be converted into a favorable scalar score. The architecture therefore avoids the category error in which aggregate benefit cancels a prohibited or threshold-level rights violation.

### 2.3 Tail-Risk Constraint

TRC remains distinct from CSV. It uses scenario families, explicit probability provenance, anti-omission floors, CVaR calculation, catastrophe-profile non-dilution, and emergency-mode constraints. `TRC_NOT_TRIGGERED` is a qualified state requiring an explicit trigger-assessment record; it is not equivalent to missing analysis.

### 2.4 Containment and Structural Viability

CSV evaluates whether a qualified option can be governed, monitored, reversed, contained, sustained, and executed without hiding burdens in unsupported institutions or human labor. The current line correctly separates procedure completion from demonstrated protective effectiveness and includes material-obligation continuity, carrier nonperformance, succession, control change, and hidden-human-compensation checks.

### 2.5 RippleLogic Score

RLS ranks only options that survive qualification. The 7 x 7 field remains profile-first: scalar summaries do not erase subgroup, worst-affected-group, rights, tail-risk, uncertainty, evidence-maturity, or non-decisiveness information. The decisiveness rule can refuse deterministic selection when uncertainty overlaps; this is a strength rather than a computational gap.

### 2.6 Sentience Gradient Protocol

SGP v8.4 preserves protection-versus-authority separation, the Human FPP-100 plateau, uncertainty-sensitive evidence states, and the distinction among computational, functional, epistemic, and phenomenal claims. It neither grants moral status from behavioral fluency nor categorically denies possible artificial experience without evidence. It remains a protection protocol, not a universal consciousness detector.

### 2.7 Calculability and machine interfaces

The framework's calculable layer is now consistently connected across Canon, companion standards, schemas, validators, examples, test vectors, and workbooks. The audit verified:

- exact release-bound run-record schema v3 identity;
- positive and adversarial run-record vectors;
- a schema-valid v3 reproducibility walkthrough and JSON record;
- SGP RMCP v8.4 and WDBIP v1.5 schemas and vectors;
- state-transition and canonical-token registries;
- live TRC, rights, PLSS, CSV, and RLS workbook interfaces;
- 2,948 Aligners Sheet formulas, 2,352 serialized results, 596 independently audited intentional blanks, zero cached formula errors, and forced full recalculation metadata;
- an RLS validation workbook whose visible title now correctly identifies it as the v0.3 Level 1 study instrument.

A framework can be fully specified yet still decline to calculate a determinate answer when evidence or authority is insufficient. `UNKNOWN`, `REFUSE`, `NOT_TRIGGERED`, `PASS_WITH_CONTROLS`, and non-decisive states are typed outputs, not missing implementation.
