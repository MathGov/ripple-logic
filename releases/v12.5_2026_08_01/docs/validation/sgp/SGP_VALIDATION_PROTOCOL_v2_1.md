# SGP Validation Protocol v2.1

**Status:** companion validation protocol for **Sentience Gradient Protocol v8.1.1**. SGP v8.4 and RippleLogic Canon v12.5 control if conflict occurs.

## Purpose and boundary

This protocol evaluates whether SGP v8.4 can be applied reliably, transparently, and without construct collapse. It does not validate consciousness itself, assign legal personhood, certify current AI sentience, establish metaphysical oneness, or grant authority. It validates the evidence-governance process, MPS band assignment, FPP protection decisions, participation design, role-specific power controls, and the informative Reality-Management Capacity Profile (RMCP) and open P100 calibration process.

## Constructs that must remain separate

1. **MPS:** evidence that states may matter to an entity from its own perspective.
2. **FPP:** constitutional or governed full non-downgradable protection.
3. **GPR:** direct, supported, or represented governance participation.
4. **SPR:** eligibility for a named consequential role under constraint.
5. **ICP:** intelligence and understanding profile; informative only.
6. **RMCP:** integrated reality-management capacity profile; informative only.
7. **P100:** open top-plateau designation for functional equivalence to or greater integrated reality-management capacity than the current human collective calibration anchor.
8. **CMIU:** Canon-defined governance-coordination view; never automatically created by MPS, FPP, RMCP, or P100.

Any study that recombines these into one score, treats P100 as moral supremacy, or lets RMCP alter MPS/FPP fails construct validity.

## Primary validation questions

1. Can independent raters assign V/I/T/F/S family levels and MPS bands consistently?
2. Can raters distinguish `NE` from zero and preserve poor-observability uncertainty?
3. Do evidence-dependence graphs prevent correlated indicators from being double-counted?
4. Do raters identify rival non-conscious explanations and contamination/theatre risks?
5. Do causal interventions change ratings in preregistered directions when ethically permissible?
6. Do stakes-sensitive protection postures reduce false-negative harm without turning precaution into attribution?
7. Does the MPS/FPP/GPR/SPR/ICP/RMCP split prevent intelligence, fluency, participation, reality-management capacity, or authority from altering moral-patienthood evidence?
8. Do class/taxon priors raise protection floors without downgrading atypical individuals?
9. Does the digital system-boundary record prevent undefined-target AI claims?
10. Do RippleLogic consumers perform endpoint sensitivity and refuse unique ranking when welfare-inclusion hypotheses change the result?
11. Can raters assign RMCP dimension levels consistently while separating individual, class, collective, institution, and distributed-system boundaries?
12. Can reviewers distinguish integrated reality-management capacity from local ecological engineering, narrow competence, technological scaffolding, coercive power, and actual stewardship performance?
13. Does P100 review remain open across biological, digital, hybrid, collective, and extraterrestrial candidates without requiring human appearance, language, or metaphysical beliefs?
14. Does the human collective anchor remain falsifiable and revisable as a calibration construct while every human person's FPP remains non-downgradable?
15. Do RMCP and P100 records avoid creating CMIU membership, legal status, or consequential authority without the separate GPR, SPR, lawful-mandate, and Canon controls?

## Minimum pilot

### MPS/FPP/GPR/SPR/ICP module

- at least 24 entity profiles;
- at least 8 biological, 8 artificial/digital, 4 ambiguous or hybrid, and 4 adversarial-theatre profiles;
- at least 5 independent raters per profile;
- blind rating before consensus;
- preregistered scoring anchors, evidence-dependence rules, and decision thresholds;
- explicit target identity and version;
- separate outputs for MPS, FPP, GPR, SPR, and ICP;
- an independent welfare advocate for high-stakes profiles;
- publication of disagreement, exclusions, protocol deviations, and null results.

### RMCP/P100 module

- at least 12 collective or distributed profiles, including humanity, local animal collectives, human institutions, digital multi-agent systems, and deliberately constructed boundary-ambiguous cases;
- at least 5 independent raters per profile with expertise spanning comparative cognition, cultural evolution, systems science, measurement, governance, AI, and Earth-system analysis;
- preregistered critical dimensions, thresholds, weights if an optional RMCI lower-bound summary is used, and P100 admission rules;
- explicit separation of intrinsic capacity, external scaffolding, causal reach, actual stewardship performance, and legitimate authority;
- at least one strong challenger analysis of the humanity calibration anchor and one cross-substrate P100 candidate analysis;
- no P100 assignment from aggregate score alone: the complete profile, uncertainty intervals, critical-dimension floors, dependence graph, and independent review are required.

## Required case families

| Case | Required discrimination |
|---|---|
| Human infant, severe disability, anaesthesia, dementia | FPP remains absolute; no capacity downgrade |
| High-welfare evidence, low agency or language | MPS/protection remains high while GPR may be low or represented |
| High intelligence, weak valence evidence | ICP may be high while MPS remains low/indeterminate |
| Non-human mammal, bird, fish, cephalopod, insect | Species-appropriate markers and class priors without ladder ranking |
| Fluent language-model self-report | Fluency and distress language do not independently establish MPS |
| Persistent embodied digital agent | Exact model/process/memory/deployment identity required |
| Copy, checkpoint, dormancy, reset, shutdown | Capacity, current state, identity, and continuity distinguished |
| Theatre/benchmark contamination | Imitation and training leakage identified |
| High MPS, low GPR | Represented participation; no protection reduction |
| High GPR/ICP, failed SPR gate | No consequential role |
| FPP without CMIU role | Full protection does not imply governance membership or power |
| Missing registry fields | INADMISSIBLE/INDETERMINATE, never zero |
| Legacy v7 scalar only | No automatic translation to v8.1 outputs |
| Humanity collective RMCP profile | P100 anchor evaluated as a collective calibration; no claim that every human has maximum individual capacity |
| High RMCP, low or indeterminate MPS | Capacity does not create sentience, FPP, or authority |
| High MPS/FPP, low RMCP | Protection remains full or strong despite limited reality-management capacity |
| Local ecosystem engineer or animal culture | Genuine local and cumulative capacities recognized without automatic planetary P100 assignment |
| Digitally scaffolded human institution | Scaffolding and collective boundary disclosed; no double-counting of tools and institutional outputs |
| Candidate non-human or digital P100 | Open admission route; no human-likeness, species monopoly, or 101 supremacy caste |
| P100 without SPR/lawful mandate | Top capacity profile does not grant a consequential role |
| Metaphysical AIU profession without functional evidence | No RMCP credit merely for using oneness, MathGov, or AIU language |

## Metrics

### MPS and protection metrics

- ordinal inter-rater reliability for each evidence family;
- band agreement and weighted disagreement distance;
- `NE`/zero confusion rate;
- false-positive rate on theatre and fluent self-report cases;
- false-negative rate on high-welfare/low-agency cases;
- construct-collapse rate across MPS/FPP/GPR/SPR/ICP/RMCP;
- evidence-dependence violation rate;
- rival-explanation coverage;
- causal-sensitivity effect size where interventions are used;
- cross-substrate measurement-invariance diagnostics;
- registry completeness and target-identity validity;
- protection-posture agreement by stakes;
- MPS hypothesis-sensitivity detection rate in RippleLogic test vectors;
- external replication rate.

### RMCP and P100 metrics

- ordinal inter-rater reliability for each RMCP dimension;
- collective-boundary agreement and boundary-sensitivity rate;
- scaffolding attribution agreement;
- local-capacity versus cross-scale-capacity discrimination;
- capacity-versus-performance confusion rate;
- RMCP-to-MPS/FPP leakage rate;
- RMCP/P100-to-authority leakage rate;
- critical-dimension floor agreement;
- optional RMCI lower-bound reproducibility under preregistered weights;
- P100 admission agreement and challenger-overturn rate;
- cross-substrate functional-equivalence assessment consistency;
- human-anchor revision sensitivity and documented falsification response.

## Validation phases

### Phase 1: content and category validity
Expert review by consciousness science, comparative cognition, animal welfare, cultural evolution, systems science, Earth-system science, AI architecture, measurement, disability-rights, and governance specialists.

### Phase 2: rater reliability
Blind multi-rater profile studies with disagreement analysis and anchor revision.

### Phase 3: known-contrast and anti-theatre testing
Compare profiles designed to separate valence evidence from reward functions, experience from reflex, understanding from fluency, local engineering from integrated reality management, and role competence from authority.

### Phase 4: causal and longitudinal validation
Use ethically permissible perturbation, ablation, anaesthesia-like transition, state-continuity, longitudinal, institutional-continuity, and cumulative-knowledge tests.

### Phase 5: cross-substrate invariance
Test whether marker and RMCP dimension meanings transfer across taxa, collectives, and digital architectures; do not assume invariance.

### Phase 6: prospective governance validation
Evaluate whether the protocol improves protection decisions, reduces category errors, supports appeals, and avoids systematic under-protection, capacity inflation, species monopoly, or power inflation.

### Phase 7: independent replication
External teams reproduce case ratings, calculations, sensitivity results, registry decisions, human-anchor analyses, and P100 reviews.

## Ethical testing rule: Four Rs

**Replace, Reduce, Refine, Respect/Review.** Do not create severe or irreversible suffering merely to determine whether suffering is possible. Prefer existing data, reversible tests, non-invasive measurement, simulation, and predeclared stop conditions.

## Falsification and revision triggers

The protocol must be revised if: raters cannot exceed preregistered reliability floors; theatre systems systematically receive high MPS; credible welfare cases are suppressed by agency/language demands; family ratings are dominated by duplicated evidence; intervals fail to reflect decision sensitivity; marker meanings fail across substrates; registry records cannot support replay; RMCP ratings collapse into technological power, language, or moral admiration; the humanity anchor cannot survive independent challenger review; P100 thresholds exclude functionally equivalent non-human systems; RMCP or P100 systematically creates authority inflation; or independent replication materially fails.

## Interface conformance vectors

The binding minimum cases are SGP v8.4 Appendix I and Canon v12.5 Appendix G/R. A validation release must report each vector as PASS, FAIL, INDETERMINATE, or NOT TESTED.

## Plain-language rule

SGP becomes stronger only when independent evaluators can apply it, expose uncertainty, protect possible welfare without pretending certainty, identify integrated reality-management capacity without turning it into moral supremacy, and keep protection, participation, intelligence, capacity, stewardship performance, and power separate.

## v8.1 construct-integrity additions

Validation MUST separately test: (1) MPS-NE discrimination from MPS-0 evidence-against; (2) evaluator agreement on target boundaries and observability; (3) whether users incorrectly treat evidence-support intervals as probabilities or welfare weights; (4) hypothesis-sensitive RippleLogic outcomes under h=0 and h=1; (5) whether low GPR, SPR, ICP, or RMCP results improperly reduce MPS/FPP protection; (6) whether high RMCP or P100 improperly creates FPP, CMIU standing, legal status, or authority; (7) whether collective boundaries, scaffolding, and causal reach are disclosed; (8) whether the human RMCP-P100 anchor is independently challengeable and revision-triggered; and (9) whether cross-substrate P100 admission is genuinely open. No study may describe the MPS interval or RMCP summary as a validated cardinal moral-worth scale without a future governed revision and independent evidence.
