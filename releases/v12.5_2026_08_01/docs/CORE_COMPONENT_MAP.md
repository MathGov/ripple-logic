## Governing relationship

The Canon and SGP are principal governing sources. Companion standards implement bounded interfaces. The Primer and Public Introduction are informative. The 15-file `core_15` selection contains fourteen DOCX reading mirrors plus the Aligners Sheet workbook; this map is a coordination artifact, not a sixteenth governing component.
