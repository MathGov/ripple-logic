## Active cascade

| Acronym | Expansion                            | Function                                                                                                          |
| ------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RG      | Reality Grounding                    | State what is known, unknown, evidenced, assumed, and claimable.                                                  |
| RSG     | Reality Support Gate                 | Formal shorthand linked to Reality Grounding claim support.                                                       |
| RF      | Rights Floor                         | Public rights-protection layer.                                                                                   |
| NCRC    | Non-Compensatory Rights Constraint   | Formal predicate implementing the Rights Floor. Rights failures cannot be rescued by welfare score.               |
| TRC     | Tail-Risk Constraint                 | Catastrophic, irreversible, ruinous, lock-in, or severe downside constraint.                                      |
| CSV     | Containment and Structural Viability | Tests whether the option can structurally stand with harms contained, routed, monitored, bounded, and controlled. |
| RLS     | RippleLogic Score                    | Residual welfare-ranking layer applied only after options survive RG/RF/TRC/CSV qualification.                    |

Public cascade: `RG -> RF -> TRC -> CSV -> RLS`

Formal shorthand: `RG/RSG -> RF/NCRC -> TRC -> CSV -> RLS`

## Two-phase method

| Phrase          | Meaning                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Qualify options | Phase 1. RG, RF/NCRC, TRC, and CSV decide whether an option may enter the selectable set.         |
| Rank survivors  | Phase 2. RLS ranks only options that survive qualification.                                       |
| No RLS rescue   | A high welfare score cannot rescue an option that failed Reality Grounding, RF/NCRC, TRC, or CSV. |

## Legacy SGP v7 fields

`SG_norm`, `SG_patient_norm`, `SG_measured_norm`, the A/B/C minimum scalar, and automatic plateau-to-CMIU coupling are deprecated v7.x fields. They may appear only in version-pinned historical records and are not equivalent to v8.1 MPS, FPP, GPR, SPR, ICP, RMCP, or P100.


### RMCP — Reality-Management Capacity Profile
An informative, multidimensional SGP v8.4 profile of reality-grounded modelling, cumulative knowledge, coordination, intervention breadth, cross-scale consequence tracking, and Recursive Union Awareness. RMCP is not sentience, moral worth, FPP, wise performance, or authority.

### P100 — Open Reality-Management Plateau
The open top-band designation for functional equivalence to or greater integrated reality-management capacity than the current human collective calibration anchor. P100 is not a permanent species monopoly, perfection, a metaphysical maximum, or unrestricted power.

### Recursive Union Awareness
The observable ability to represent a self or collective as causally embedded within wider systems, trace consequences across scales, and revise action or rules accordingly. It does not prove metaphysical oneness or require AIU language.

### FPP-100 / RMCP-P100 dual anchor
Every human person receives FPP-100 protection; humanity collectively serves as the provisional current RMCP-P100 reference calibration anchor. The two statuses are distinct and neither grants automatic CMIU membership or authority.

## Reproducibility terms

- **R0_RECORD_COMPLETE:** required run-record objects and valid status values are present.
- **R1_CASCADE_CONFORMANT:** the run follows the five-stage ordering and non-compensation rules.
- **R2_INDEPENDENTLY_REPLAYED:** a qualified independent implementer reproduced the declared decision state within tolerance.
- **Parameter lock:** the frozen decision-material evidence cutoff, parameters, reviewers, and amendment rules used before outcome-sensitive ranking.
- **Requalification:** reopening affected stages after a material change in evidence, scope, parameters, system behavior, or distribution.
