## Executive verdict

The feedback contained both genuine defects and major false positives caused by auditing an incomplete or transformed evidence surface rather than the supplied release ZIP. MathGov's five-stage architecture remains sound and unchanged:

**RG -> RF/NCRC -> TRC -> CSV -> RLS**

No sixth gate, eighth welfare dimension, new moral-status hierarchy, capacity-to-authority inference, or empirical-certification claim was added. The accepted corrections improve calculability, version identity, record-schema clarity, navigation, and release portability. Suggestions unsupported by the actual package were rejected.

The corrected package is suitable for public research-source release, teaching, structured Tier 1-2 use, Tier 3 procedural pilots with qualified review, and empirical-validation preparation. It remains explicitly uncertified for high-stakes deployment, legal compliance, physical or medical safety, consciousness detection, metaphysical proof, empirical superiority, ProofPack, or Tier 4.
