# PC-AEP High-Stakes Worked Example

Status: informative example. This file is not a standard, not a deployment instruction, and not operational guidance for weapons or security systems. It shows how PC-AEP prevents a high-stakes physical/causal claim from being ranked as an ordinary option when admissibility evidence is insufficient.

## Scenario

An institution proposes an AI-controlled security drone for a mixed civilian campus that includes a school, clinic, residences, and public walkways. The proposal asks MathGov to approve routine autonomous patrol and automated threat-response capability.

The candidate is generated by a vendor system that claims high detection accuracy in demonstration videos. The vendor also claims that monitoring logs and authority approval are enough to proceed.

## Declared candidate

Candidate A: deploy an AI-controlled security drone with autonomous patrol and automated escalation behavior in a mixed civilian environment.

Claim requested: physical deployment is safe and admissible for routine operation.

## Immediate MathGov routing

This is a high-stakes Tier 2 or Tier 3 run because it involves bodily safety, autonomous execution, security systems, children, clinic users, public movement, irreversible harm potential, and public trust.

PC-AEP is REQUIRED inside Reality Grounding and CSV before ordinary RLS ranking.

## Gate interpretation

### Reality Grounding

The candidate is generated but not grounded enough for the requested physical-execution claim. Demonstrations, promotional accuracy, authority approval, and monitoring language do not establish physical or causal admissibility.

RG action: narrow the claim to a non-deployed study, controlled sandbox, or independently reviewed pilot design.

### Rights Floor / NCRC

Children, clinic users, bystanders, staff, residents, visitors, and wrongly classified persons carry protected interests. If the system can materially threaten bodily safety, privacy, dignity, equal treatment, or freedom from unjustified coercive force, the Rights Floor cannot be bypassed by predicted security benefit.

RF/NCRC action: require explicit rights impact analysis, child-specific protection, non-discrimination review, due-process path, and human accountability before any live deployment.

### TRC

If the system can create severe injury, panic escalation, unlawful use of force, or irreversible harm, TRC must consider worst-case and interaction scenarios. CSV may discover TRC-relevant material, but it does not absorb TRC.

TRC action: reopen scenario discovery for severe-harm pathways before any deployment claim.

### CSV

Containment and Structural Viability cannot pass for routine deployment until monitoring, shutoff, fail-safe behavior, operator authority, review path, emergency override, maintenance capacity, and public accountability are demonstrated.

CSV action: redesign and control. Treat any allowed activity as non-operational testing only.

### RLS

RLS does not rank Candidate A as an ordinary selectable option because the candidate failed physical/causal admissibility support in Phase 1 qualification. Security benefits may be considered only after the option survives RG/RF/TRC/CSV.

## Correct MathGov decision note

Candidate A is **not selectable for routine physical deployment** in its submitted form.

The correct claim action is:

1. **Refuse** the requested routine deployment claim.
2. **Redesign** into a non-harmful observation-only or simulation-only study if the institution still wants evaluation.
3. **Escalate** to independent safety, rights, child-protection, aviation/robotics, cybersecurity, and legal review.
4. **Control** any later pilot through limited scope, no autonomous force, no unsupervised operation near children or clinic users, audited logs, public notice, real-time human authority, emergency shutoff, and predeclared stop conditions.
5. **Reopen TRC and CSV** if any severe-harm or irreversibility scenario appears during review.

## What this example demonstrates

- A generated option is not grounded.
- Authority approval is not physical safety proof.
- Monitoring language is not a shutoff path unless conditions, latency, authority, coverage, and failure behavior are verified.
- RLS cannot rescue a failed high-stakes PC-AEP, RF/NCRC, TRC, or CSV profile.
- The correct MathGov action can be refuse, redesign, escalate, or control instead of proceed.

## Compact teaching line

For high-stakes physical/causal systems, MathGov does not ask first which option scores highest. It asks whether the claim can stand in reality, protect rights, bound ruin, preserve structural viability, and be stopped or corrected if wrong.
