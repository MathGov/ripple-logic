## WDBIP placement

WDBIP v1.4 is included in the complete MathGov release because welfare-boundary, interaction, dependence, subgroup, and migration discipline are material to RLS evidence integrity. It is a Canon-subordinate normative implementation companion beneath RLS. It is not a third principal governing source, not an eighth welfare dimension, and not a sixth gate.

## Machine and reproducibility boundary

`schemas/mathgov_run_record_v2.schema.json` defines the minimum structural interface. `release/VALIDATE_MATHGOV_RUN.py` separates R0 schema validation, R1 semantic/state conformance, and R2 bounded use-readiness review. Passing these checks establishes only the declared structural and procedural conformance; it does not establish evidence truth, empirical validity, legal authority, physical safety, consciousness, moral truth, or Tier 4 status.
