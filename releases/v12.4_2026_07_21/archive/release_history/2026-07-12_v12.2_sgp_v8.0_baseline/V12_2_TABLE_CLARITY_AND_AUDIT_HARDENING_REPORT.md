# v12.2 Same-Version Table Clarity and Audit Hardening Report

**Release line:** MathGov Core Release 2026.09 v12.2 / SGP v8.0  
**Completed:** 12 July 2026  
**Version decision:** No component version number changed.

## Verdict

The black-and-white tables in the Canon, Agent System, Primer, compact standards, validation protocol, public introduction, and component map were a production-pipeline inconsistency rather than a deliberate semantic distinction. SGP v8.0 had explicit cell shading, border, padding, and header formatting, while most other DOCX reading copies inherited a plain Word table style. The source content was linked, but the visual language was not.

This pass applies one consistent reading-copy table system across all 13 DOCX and PDF mirrors:

- dark-blue header rows (`#1F4E78`);
- white bold header text;
- alternating white and pale-blue body rows;
- blue borders and compact cell padding;
- top-aligned, wrapped text;
- readable column-sensitive type sizing;
- repeated header rows across page breaks;
- preserved table content, equations, labels, and component versions.

The same-version audit also integrated only feedback that was confirmed against the supplied source files and that materially improved construct clarity, executable consistency, or claim discipline.

## Accurate findings integrated

1. **Current-line pin cleanup.** Stale current labels inside the workbook and reader surfaces were synchronized to Canon v12.2, SGP v8.0, ripple.md v5.2, Agent System v12.0, Primer v4.0, and Aligners Sheet v5.2. Historical rows remain historical.
2. **Three-channel NCRC compact interface.** The compact equation surfaces now preserve floor, categorical, and severe-hazard channels plus unresolved-evidence behavior and the emergency comparison tuple.
3. **Composite CSV resolver.** UCI is treated as one possible subcondition rather than the whole CSV gate. The compact resolver now includes containment integrity, structural viability, source coupling, physical/causal admissibility, dependency and lock-in, monitoring, reversibility, controls, legitimacy, and evidence sufficiency.
4. **SGP binding flag registration.** `SGP_BINDING_INTEGRITY_FAIL` is defined in the controlling audit-flag registry.
5. **Rights-cell zero discipline.** The workbook now includes `Rights_Evidence_Disposition`, covering all 70 active right-cell memberships. A numerical zero requires an explicit evidence disposition; blank or unreviewed is unknown, not zero.
6. **Source debt routed into CSV.** The workbook records source-coupling status, materiality, binding controls, owners, monitoring triggers, failure consequences, review timing, and CSV routing effect.
7. **UCI applicability correction.** Inapplicable Equity components for U1 and U7 are `NA` and are excluded through renormalization. Missing applicable evidence remains `UNKNOWN` and is not assigned a perfect value.
8. **Decision-specific containment graph.** Containing relationships are declared for the worked decision rather than inferred solely from U1–U7 row order.
9. **TRC severity and dependence disclosure.** Scenario partitions, driver clusters, C1–C4 severity classes, C3/C4 reopening conditions, and the non-calibrated-prior boundary are explicit.
10. **RLS dependence and distribution hardening.** The Canon and workbook identify shared uncertainty clusters, perfect-correlation stress, covariance-aware future use, worst-affected subgroup reporting, burden concentration, and non-decisiveness when dependence is not resolved.
11. **Confidence and uncertainty separation.** Confidence shrinkage and uncertainty intervals are no longer described as interchangeable.
12. **Peak-harm companion.** Short, intense harms require peak-severity sensitivity where decision-material rather than being hidden by duration integration alone.
13. **U6 view tags.** `U6_HUMANITY`, `U6_COORDINATION`, and `U6_MIXED_WITH_DECOMPOSITION` prevent human-welfare and coordination-capacity claims from being silently combined.
14. **PLSS sensitivity disclosure.** The workbook now discloses the declared direct-q operator, uniform fallback, pending geometric-mean and broadened-guardrail comparisons, and the resulting review boundary.
15. **Agent self-audit correction.** Gate-critical unknowns can no longer be narrated as ordinary zero/pass outcomes; the example is narrowed, bounded, or escalated as required.
16. **Claim-language precision.** Overbroad statements about Arrow’s theorem, expected value, and mathematically “forced” non-averagability were narrowed to their actual assumptions and framework commitments.
17. **ripple.md wrapper clarity.** Non-Domination and Epistemic Integrity remain wrapper obligations that may block or narrow a run but do not become new Canon cascade levels.
18. **MPS-4 and FPP separation.** Strong evidence and governed full protection remain distinct everywhere.
