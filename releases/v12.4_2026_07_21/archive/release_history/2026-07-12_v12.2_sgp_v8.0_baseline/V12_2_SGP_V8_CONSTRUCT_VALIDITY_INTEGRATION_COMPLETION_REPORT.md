## Governing correction

SGP v7.x stated that protection, participation, intelligence, and authority were distinct, but its active `min(Awareness, Agency, Union Participation)` scalar still combined those constructs. SGP v8.0 corrects the type system itself:

- **MPS** governs evidence of welfare-bearing experience;
- **FPP** governs full non-downgradable rights-of-protection;
- **GPR** governs direct, supported, or represented participation;
- **SPR** governs eligibility for a named consequential role;
- **ICP** records intelligence and understanding capacities without changing moral patienthood by itself.

This prevents low agency, language, metacognition, ethical participation, or governance competence from being misread as reduced capacity to suffer.
