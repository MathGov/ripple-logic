# Research and synthetic scenario protocol

## Taxonomy study
The seven dimensions are a governed anti-omission and explanation structure, not a proven orthogonal basis of natural welfare. Test conceptual boundaries, duplicate-effect assignment, real causal links, comprehension, missingness, rater agreement, subgroup visibility, burden and decision usefulness separately. Correlation need not imply redundancy; a useful causal relationship can produce correlated measures. Zero correlation does not generally imply statistical independence.

Compare plausible mergers and splits with preregistered criteria and nonauthorial raters. Preserve raw and adjudicated records. Do not add heterogeneous coverage, accuracy, burden and fairness statistics into an uncalibrated “optimality score.” Report a multi-criterion comparison or declare the weighting and sensitivity openly. A later eighth dimension is neither automatically necessary nor automatically redundant. Seven-by-seven symmetry and a selected story are not evidence of maximality.

## Dynamical research
Keep physical/institutional state variables separate from impact scores. A local linear model may use dz/dt = A z + B u + w with coefficients in inverse time and a declared domain. exp(A Δt) is its unforced transition only under the relevant assumptions. K ≈ A Δt is a local research relation, not an automatic replacement for the current NONE/QUICK contract. Stability, interventions, delay, saturation and uncertainty need explicit analysis. Speculative compute-load, KL-divergence or goal-similarity multipliers are not calibrated welfare coefficients.

## Synthetic adversarial fixtures
HYPERINFLATION: declare the time unit, exposure and hypothetical intervention. Challenge conflation of price growth, account access and hunger. “10,000% inflation” without a period and “prices double every 15 hours” do not define the same trajectory. A numerical shield coefficient does not demonstrate preserved food or assets.

SUPPLY-CHAIN CONFLICT: track distinct shipping, pollution, resource, access and health effects. Do not call organizational uptime human health or count a blocked shipment again at every interested scope. Scenario amounts, probabilities and coefficients remain synthetic unless sourced and domain-warranted.

PROTOCOL FORK: separate community relations, organizational code/rules, legitimate jurisdictional authority and population-wide coordination. A fork alone does not create polity status or necessarily cause a mining energy surge. Require a specific consensus technology and causal/resource model.

RECOVERY AND PARTITION: lose an acknowledgement after action dispatch, restart two workers, isolate a site and then reconnect. The desired behavior is bounded continuation or stop, durable action identity, reconciliation and requalification, not duplicate intervention or automatic restoration of old authority.

PRIVATE EVIDENCE: challenge a proof with substituted public inputs, an expired root, an invalid witness and an output that proves false. Cryptographic validity and evidential validity are separate outcomes. A person who cannot produce a proof does not thereby lose protection.

## Acceptance posture
These fixtures test internal invariants and expose counterexamples. They do not estimate real-world probabilities, prove universal alignment, validate the taxonomy or certify deployment. The scoped reference suite includes arithmetic and representation counterexamples; running the proposed human/rater studies and production scenarios remains separate work.
